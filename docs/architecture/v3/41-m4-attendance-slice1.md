Routes (all under `/api/v1/attendance`):

- `POST /sessions` (`attendance.session.mark`, `Idempotency-Key`): a teacher with a faculty assignment valid on `sessionDate` creates a session. The teacher recorded is the caller's own employee row; the body cannot name teacher, campus or state.
- `POST /sessions/:id/attendance` (`attendance.session.mark`, `Idempotency-Key`): first submission. The body must name exactly the students holding a live registration on the session date (partial or padded list: 409). One transaction writes one record and revision 1 per student and moves the session to `CONDUCTED` / `SUBMITTED`. A second submission is a 409 under the session row lock.
- `GET /sessions/:id`, `GET /sessions/:id/records`, `GET /records/:id/revisions` (`attendance.record.read`).

## 2. Rules and where they are enforced

- **Authorization order**: staff actor, permission held at all (403), row exists, scoped assert (404, identical to "missing"). ASSIGNED proves, from server-read rows only, an ACTIVE employee with a faculty assignment on that offering covering the date, on a PUBLISHED or CLOSED offering. GRANTED (HOD department, CAMPUS_ADMIN campus, COLLEGE_ADMIN organization) uses the offering's own campus and department. A foreign tenant sees 404 (RLS plus the engine's tenant check).
- **Session guard (database)**: offering PUBLISHED and `tracks_attendance`; term ACTIVE (never LOCKED) and the date inside it; `starts_at` and `ends_at` fall on `session_date` in the campus time zone; an optional timetable slot belongs to the offering, is effective on the date and matches the ISO weekday; a REGULAR session cannot fall on an ACTIVE `HOLIDAY` or `NON_TEACHING_DAY` (EXTRA and MAKEUP can, `EVENT` never blocks); the conducting employee is ACTIVE with a valid assignment. `campus_id` is derived, never supplied.
- **Record guard (database)**: only while the session is `NOT_STARTED` or `OPEN`; revision 1; offering and date derived from the session; the registration must match student and offering and be live on the session date (not `PENDING_APPROVAL` or `REJECTED`).
- **Append-only history**: revisions reject UPDATE and DELETE for every role (the `reject_audit_mutation` function of `audit_events`); records and sessions reject DELETE. A deferred constraint trigger, fired from both tables, requires at commit that the record's `revision_no`, status, source and actor equal its latest revision and that revisions are contiguous. A record therefore cannot change without a paired revision, nor a revision without the record change. `status_before` is NULL only for revision 1 (CHECK and chain guard); a later revision must chain from the previous `status_after` and change the value.
- **Initial marking** is revision 1, `status_before` NULL (doc 02 makes it nullable), `reason_code` `OTHER`, `reason_text` "Initial marking": the closed reason list has no initial-mark value. Owner may prefer a dedicated value; that would be a later migration.
- **Student self** (doc 05 §5): RESTRICTIVE policies; a STUDENT sees only their own records, the revisions of their own records and the sessions in which they have a record, and never writes. The runtime role has no DELETE anywhere and UPDATE only on the listed columns.
- **Audit**: `attendance.session.create` and `attendance.session.submit`, risk tier 1, in the same transaction.

**Correction** (`attendance.record.correct`, `Idempotency-Key` required). Body: `status`, `expectedRevisionNo`, `reasonCode` (FACULTY_ERROR, STUDENT_DISPUTE, MEDICAL, DUTY_LEAVE, OTHER), `reasonText` (5 to 500 characters, required for OTHER). Unknown fields are rejected, and neither the source, the actor nor the time can be supplied.

- One transaction updates the record (`status`, `source = CORRECTION`, `marked_by_user_id`, `marked_at`, `revision_no + 1`), inserts revision N+1 (`status_before`, `status_after`, actor, reason) and writes an `attendance.record.correct` audit event at tier 2 (reason code and text, subject student, old and new status and revision).
- Concurrency: the session is locked FOR SHARE, then the record FOR UPDATE (the order submission uses; cancel takes the session FOR UPDATE, so the two cannot interleave). `expectedRevisionNo` must equal the current revision, otherwise 409, so two concurrent corrections cannot both apply. The same key sent twice at once writes once.
- Refused with 409 and no change: session not SUBMITTED, session LOCKED, term LOCKED (database guard), stale revision, or a status equal to the current one.
- Any status may be corrected to any other (the canonical documents define no restriction).
- Authorization: 403 when the permission is not held at all; otherwise the ASSIGNED proof on the session's offering and date (an assignment that has ended or not started on that date, another tenant, and a missing record all return the same 404).

**Cancellation** (`attendance.session.mark`, `Idempotency-Key` required). Body: `reason` (5 to 500 characters). SCHEDULED to CANCELLED with `cancel_reason`; allowed only while no attendance was submitted; a conducted session cannot be cancelled (its attendance changes by correction); a cancelled session is final and takes no records. Audited as `attendance.session.cancel` (tier 1).

Database backing (from 0040): records move only with a paired revision (deferred check, both directions), revisions are append-only for every role, a LOCKED session or term is read-only, session status moves only SCHEDULED to CONDUCTED or CANCELLED.

## 7. Canonical gaps and owner questions (nothing here was decided silently)

1. **Who holds `attendance.record.correct`.** Doc 03 names the permission but no role table, doc 10 matrix or sensitivity assigns it. FACULTY ASSIGNED is provisional, by analogy with `attendance.session.mark` and doc 01 §3.7 ("changes only via revision", session owner "Faculty (own), admin"). No admin role holds it. Owner to confirm, including whether HOD or campus admins may correct.
2. **Edit window and approval.** Doc 02 and doc 07 §4 put `edit_window_days` and `correction_requires_approval` in `attendance_policies`, and `terms.attendance_edit_until` was deferred to M4. None of these exists yet, so corrections are allowed at any time while the session is SUBMITTED and the term is not LOCKED. A policy slice must add the window and the approval path (`approved_by_user_id`, `service_request_id`, M5 service requests).
3. **Session locking and unlocking.** Doc 03 lists "lock/unlock" at audit tier 3 and "mark edit after lock" as a CRITICAL action needing a reason and optionally a second approver, but defines no permission, no actor, no automatic trigger (edit window end, term lock) and no unlock rule. The database already supports SUBMITTED to LOCKED. No lock or unlock endpoint was built.
4. **Cancel permission.** No canonical permission governs cancellation, so it reuses `attendance.session.mark`. Admin cancellation is therefore not possible. A dedicated permission or admin path needs the owner's decision.
5. **Reason text.** Doc 02 makes `reason_text` optional; this slice requires it only for OTHER.
6. **Events.** `attendance.record_corrected` (doc 03 §events) is not emitted: the outbox and notification consumers are not built.
7. **Summaries.** Corrections do not recompute `attendance_summaries`: that table is not built.

### 12.2 Permissions and migration

`0042` registers `attendance.correction.approve` (SENSITIVE), `attendance.session.lock` (CRITICAL), `attendance.session.cancel` (SENSITIVE), adds `approved_by_user_id` and `correction_request_id` (nullable, paired, approver never equals changer) to `attendance_record_revisions`, `locked_by_user_id` and `lock_reason` to `class_sessions`, and the two tables above, both under forced row level security (`attendance_policies`: tenant select, staff only, no writes; `attendance_correction_requests`: tenant, staff only, no student access). Role grants come from the tenant role seed (`role-matrix.ts`; doc 10 rows 60 to 63). **Existing tenants are not re-seeded** (separate, unauthorised production operation); a tenant seeded before this change holds no correction, approval, lock or cancel grant for any role, so those routes stay closed there. Permission count 53 to 56.

### 12.3 Fail-closed switch

`ATTENDANCE_CORRECTIONS_ENABLED` stays default `false` and now guards the correction, correction-request, decision and list routes (403 before any lookup). Locking and cancellation are not behind it. Enabling it anywhere other than a disposable synthetic environment is a deployment decision that needs the owner's acceptance; this slice does not make that decision.

### 12.4 Decisions the approval left open (safest reading chosen)

1. "Regular assigned faculty": `PRIMARY` and `CO_INSTRUCTOR`; others are excluded (AD-1 says not automatic).
2. "Canonical permitted-status rules": any change to a different status with a reason from the closed list; `SYSTEM_RECOMPUTE` stays system-only; EXCUSED has no extra rule yet (leave records do not exist).
3. Window unit: calendar days from the campus-local session date, day 3 inclusive.
4. Which administrators lock and cancel: CAMPUS_ADMIN and COLLEGE_ADMIN only. **HOD does not lock or cancel** (AD-5 names administrators; AD-7 says "separate explicit permission"). Widening to HOD is a one-line matrix change the owner can ask for.
5. Who approves: HOD, CAMPUS_ADMIN, COLLEGE_ADMIN within scope, never the requester. No second approver, no M5 service-request link yet.
6. Approval applies the change exactly as requested; if the record moved on, the approver rejects and a new request is made.
7. Administrators can correct directly inside the window in an ACTIVE or CLOSED term; outside it they need an independent approval like everyone else.
