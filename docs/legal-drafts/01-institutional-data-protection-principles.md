# 01 — Institutional Data Protection Principles (draft)

> **DRAFT — NOT LEGALLY APPROVED.** Prepared for owner and qualified-counsel review. Not published, not offered to any school, not a promise. Every clause tagged _[Contract]_, _[Process]_ or _[Technical]_ (doc 46 §2); _[Technical]_ may be stated to a school only where the cited control exists and passes its test. Bracketed items are open decisions.

1. **The institution owns and controls its data.** _[Contract]_ The institution decides why its students', guardians' and staff members' personal data is processed; Anesa processes it only on the institution's documented instructions to provide the agreed service. [Controller/processor allocation to be confirmed by counsel.]
2. **Purpose limitation.** _[Contract]_ No use for advertising, profiling, resale, model training, benchmarking or product analytics that identifies a person or an institution, without separate written authorization that the law permits.
3. **Minimisation.** _[Technical, partly]_ Only fields the institution registers are collected (ADR-020); notifications carry no student data; sensitive import cells are not stored (doc 26).
4. **Isolation.** _[Technical]_ Each institution's records are separated from every other institution's by row-level security and an authorization engine, tested continuously. This does **not** by itself prevent access by Anesa's privileged operators (doc 46 §4).
5. **Restricted operator access.** _[Contract + Process; Technical when the platform realm exists]_ No standing Anesa access to institutional records; support access only by a time-boxed, justified, institution-approved, audited grant (draft 06).
6. **Children first.** _[Contract + Process]_ Processing of under-18 data follows counsel's written advice on consent, exemptions and monitoring limits. [Open: doc 46 §8.]
7. **Transparency.** _[Contract]_ Hosting location and every sub-processor are disclosed (draft 04); changes are notified in advance.
8. **Return and deletion.** _[Contract + Process]_ The institution can take its data in a documented, verified format when it leaves, and eligible data is deleted afterwards with a completion record (draft 05).
9. **Accountability.** _[Process]_ Incidents are handled and notified per draft 07; the institution can obtain evidence of controls (audit extracts, test reports) on request.
