# 03 — Data Processing Agreement outline (draft)

> **DRAFT — NOT LEGALLY APPROVED.** Prepared for owner and qualified-counsel review. Not published, not offered to any school, not a promise. Every clause tagged _[Contract]_, _[Process]_ or _[Technical]_ (doc 46 §2); _[Technical]_ may be stated to a school only where the cited control exists and passes its test. Bracketed items are open decisions.

Parties, roles [controller/fiduciary = institution; processor = Anesa; counsel to confirm]. Clauses to include:

1. Subject matter, duration, nature and purpose; categories of data and data subjects (inventory: doc 46 §3).
2. Processing only on documented instructions; no sale or unrelated use (draft 02); no AI training.
3. Confidentiality of personnel; named roles with access; background checks [decision].
4. Security measures: tenant isolation, authorization, MFA for administrators, encryption of secrets, audit logging, backups, key management; **explicit statement of what operators can technically access and the controls over it** (doc 46 §4); annex of controls with their evidence.
5. Sub-processors: list (draft 04), prior notice period [days], objection and termination right, flow-down terms.
6. Location and cross-border transfers: regions; no transfer outside [list] without consent; AI/e-mail/SMS providers.
7. Assistance with data-principal requests (access, correction, erasure, grievance) within [days].
8. Personal data breach: notice to the institution without undue delay and within [hours], content, cooperation (draft 07).
9. Audit and inspection: documentation, third-party reports, on-site/remote audits, frequency.
10. Return and deletion on termination: export format, transition period, deletion timeline, backup expiry window, completion certificate (draft 05).
11. Retention schedule per data class [institution/legal decision].
12. Liability, indemnity, insurance [counsel]; governing law and jurisdiction [counsel].
13. Annexes: processing details, security controls, sub-processors, contacts, retention schedule.
