# 05 — Data Export and Offboarding Policy (draft)

> **DRAFT — NOT LEGALLY APPROVED.** Prepared for owner and qualified-counsel review. Not published, not offered to any school, not a promise. Every clause tagged _[Contract]_, _[Process]_ or _[Technical]_ (doc 46 §2); _[Technical]_ may be stated to a school only where the cited control exists and passes its test. Bracketed items are open decisions.

1. **Right to export.** _[Contract]_ During the term and the transition period the institution may export its data. _[Technical: not built; until it is, a manual dual-control procedure applies — doc 46 §6.]_
2. **Who and how.** Organization administrators with MFA and fresh step-up; a second administrator approves a full export _[proposal]_.
3. **Contents and format.** Versioned bundle with manifest, checksums, data dictionary, files; security secrets are never included (doc 46 §5).
4. **Delivery.** Encrypted; key sent separately; link expires after [days]; every request and download is audited and notified to all organization administrators.
5. **Termination steps.** Written notice by authorised signatory → authority verification → transition period of [days] → export and written receipt → staged access revocation → deletion/anonymization of eligible data → backup expiry → completion certificate.
6. **Retention exceptions.** Only records the institution or the law requires, listed in the certificate [legal advice; no period is invented here].
7. **Backups.** Deleted data remains in backups until rotation, not longer than [window]; backups are not restored for any other purpose.
8. **Fees and timelines.** [commercial decision].
9. **Dispute or legal hold.** Deletion is paused only for a documented legal hold and the institution is informed.
