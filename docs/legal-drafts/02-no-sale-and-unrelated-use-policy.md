# 02 — No Sale or Unrelated Use of Institutional Personal Data (draft policy)

> **DRAFT — NOT LEGALLY APPROVED.** Prepared for owner and qualified-counsel review. Not published, not offered to any school, not a promise. Every clause tagged _[Contract]_, _[Process]_ or _[Technical]_ (doc 46 §2); _[Technical]_ may be stated to a school only where the cited control exists and passes its test. Bracketed items are open decisions.

**Scope.** All personal data Anesa processes for an institution (students, guardians, staff, applicants, visitors) and derived data (logs, audit records, analytics).

**Prohibited** _[Contract]_ unless the institution has given a separate, written, revocable authorization that applicable law permits: selling, renting or sharing for consideration; advertising or marketing to data principals; behavioural tracking or profiling of children; training, fine-tuning or evaluating machine-learning models on institutional data; combining data across institutions; using identifiable data for sales demos or research; disclosure to any third party other than a disclosed sub-processor or as legally compelled (with notice to the institution where lawful).

**Permitted.** Operating, securing, supporting, backing up and improving the service for that institution using aggregated, de-identified operational metrics that cannot identify a person or an institution [counsel to define de-identification standard].

**Technical status.** Today the application makes no outbound call except to object storage and the AI provider defaults to a disabled adapter _[Technical, contingent: true of current code, not a guarantee]_. Enforcement is otherwise by contract, privileged-access controls (draft 06) and audit.

**Breach of policy.** [Contract remedies, termination right, notification duty: counsel.]
